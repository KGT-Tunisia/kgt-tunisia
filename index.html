01 / ABOUT

Who is KGT?

KGT — Kinetic Guard Tunisia is a Tunisian cybersecurity and digital-awareness initiative. Our public mission is to share practical security knowledge, encourage safer digital habits and explore technology responsibly.

KGT does not promote unauthorized access, disruption, data theft or attacks against systems. Security testing should be performed only with explicit authorization from the system owner or responsible organization.
